FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Is it really offline?

Yes. The only time an internet connection is required is the first download of an AI model — around 75 MB for the tiny model. After that, transcription works completely offline, even in airplane mode.

Windows shows "Windows protected your PC" — is that normal?

Yes. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen warns about every new publisher. Click More info, then Run anyway to continue installing.

Does it need administrator rights?

No. LipiVoice Pro installs per user into your own AppData folder, so there is no UAC prompt and no admin password required.

Which languages are supported?

Dozens of languages, including Bengali, English, Hindi, Urdu, Arabic, Spanish, French, German, Chinese, Japanese, Korean and more. Auto Detect identifies the language for you.

Can it translate Bengali audio into English?

Yes. Enable Translate to English and the app transcribes in the source language first, then translates the output into English.

Is there a file size or length limit?

No limit is imposed by the app. Processing time depends on your CPU and the model you choose — roughly a minute for ten minutes of audio with the tiny model.

How do I get updates?

Updates are included. When a new version is released, download the latest installer from your original purchase page at no extra cost.

How do I uninstall it?

Go to Settings → Apps → LipiVoice Pro → Uninstall. Your transcripts in the Documents folder are kept.
